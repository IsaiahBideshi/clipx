# v1.3.0 Clip your games in ClipX

ClipX can now record clips by itself.

1. Open the Recording tab and check Clipping on. The first time, ClipX downloads what it needs to record. If the box is greyed out, choose a clips folder in Settings first.
2. Click the Hotkey button and press the keys you want to clip with.
3. Set the clip length.
4. While you play, press your hotkey to save the last few seconds. The clip shows up in Local Files.

On the Recording page you can also pick which screens to record, give your mic or apps like Discord their own audio track, and choose the game for apps ClipX doesn't recognize.
